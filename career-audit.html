Good developers don't always get good opportunities.

Career progression in tech is rarely a pure meritocracy of technical ability. Growing your senior career requires distinct non-coding skill sets:

→ Strategic positioning for target roles
→ Resumes that highlight business impact
→ High-level technical storytelling
→ Clear long-term career direction
